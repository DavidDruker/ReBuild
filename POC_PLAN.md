# ReBuild PoC scope

This applies the core workflow from the earlier PoC plan to the existing web app, with the user's latest instructions taking precedence:

- Preserve the original main design and real photographs.
- Keep one shared experience; no seller/buyer view or role switch.
- Remove interface sections that are unnecessary for the PoC.
- Retain the original catalog; the earlier four-product seed list and specific filmed product test are not requirements.
- Offer an optional local video assessment while creating a listing; show the result as advisory evidence and keep publishing available.

## Preserve

The dark sidebar, top search, original logo, typography, green buttons, summary strip, category filters, photograph-based material cards, and modal form styling. All 30 original catalog entries remain available. Do not introduce a new hero, palette, illustration system, or navigation layout.

## Required workflow

1. Find a material or publish surplus through a blank material form.
2. Submit a quantity and pickup-date request. The same company workspace supports both requesting material and recording requests on its own listings.
3. In Activity, progress through Requested → Accepted → Ready for pickup → Completed.
4. Update remaining inventory and completed financial / estimated material totals.
5. Cancel before completion to release stock, or reset the local demo to the original catalog.

Any suitable material may be used to demonstrate this workflow. There is no mandatory product name, price, quantity, or sequence of searches.

## Remove

Separate supply and demand pages, seller/buyer switching, placeholder enterprise settings and notifications, standalone matching/logistics dashboards, simulated card payment, and hardcoded impact claims.

## Constraints

This remains a local PoC, with fictional marketplace activity and browser persistence. All handoff actions are exposed in one shared Activity page. No authentication, supplier communication, payment processing, or production authorization model is implied. Completed quantities and mass assumptions drive impact estimates; no verified environmental claims are made. Video analysis requires a locally running Ollama model and Python server; it makes no certification or verified-condition claim.

The original CSS is the visual baseline. Additional styles are limited to the required activity actions, dialog accessibility, explanatory impact details, and mobile navigation behavior.
